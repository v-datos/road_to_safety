      05 · Severity prediction

      Models estimate severity among reported collisions

      The comparison used eight recorded conditions and timing fields. LightGBM had the lowest pooled log loss among the fitted models in the reused 2020–2021 comparison. Its threshold detected 82% of fatal collisions, and 3.0% of flagged collisions were fatal.

      The model estimates fatal severity among recorded collisions with casualties. Predicting whether a crash will happen requires traffic exposure and observations of uneventful travel. The tested years were previously examined.

        07 · Practical next steps

        Where local evidence can guide action

        Design and evaluate lower-speed local streets

        Municipal road authorities can test lower posted speeds and street designs that support them on suitable local streets with pedestrian activity. Measure operating speeds and pedestrian injury collisions against comparable streets. Track pedestrian exposure and changes in traffic volume. Fridman et al., Toronto quasi-experiment (2020)

        Audit rural corridors for roadway departures

        Provincial and territorial road authorities can use local crash records to select appropriate corridors and assess centerline or shoulder rumble strips. Review shoulder width, cycling access, pavement and nearby residences before installation. Compare target fatal and injury crashes per vehicle-kilometre against similar untreated corridors over multiple years. FHWA rumble strip implementation guidance

        Audit visibility at crossings after dark

        Municipal and provincial engineers can audit crossings and intersections with documented nighttime conflicts. Assess lighting, markings, glare and sight distance together. Verify lighting performance and compare nighttime injury collisions at treated and comparable untreated sites, tracking pedestrian and vehicle volumes. FHWA proven safety countermeasure on lighting

        Link local exposure and collision reporting

        Transport ministries, police and municipal data teams can agree on a versioned road segment key, coverage fields and harmonized outcome definitions. Preserve confidentiality through controlled access and aggregated releases. Track geocoding completeness, matched exposure coverage, reporting delay and the share of severe outcomes with a verified definition. CCMTA Road Safety Strategy 2035 and Beyond
