# External collision benchmark

`tractor_collision_benchmark.csv` transcribes the **tractor-trailer** fatal and injury collision columns of Table 1 in Transport Canada's [Commercial vehicle collisions in Canada, 2012–2021](https://tc.canada.ca/en/road-transportation/statistics-data/commercial-vehicle-collisions/2021/commercial-vehicle-collisions-canada-2012-2021), accessed 2026-10-15 UTC. It counts unique collisions, unlike Table S8's vehicle involvements. The publication does not explicitly specify its operational `V_TYPE` filter or data vintage, so mismatches with public records remain visible rather than being corrected to these numbers. Row-level source provenance is in `data/sources.json` and the download manifest.

`tractor_occupant_benchmarks.csv` records **published tractor-trailer occupant deaths and injuries**, not all victims of tractor-involved collisions. It transcribes Table 12 of the [2021 commercial-vehicle report to Parliament](https://tc.canada.ca/en/road-transportation/motor-vehicle-safety/motor-carriers-commercial-vehicles-drivers/commercial-vehicle-safety-canada-2021-annual-report-parliament) (prepared May 2026), and the tractor-occupant fatality row of [2021 traffic collision statistics](https://tc.canada.ca/en/road-transportation/statistics-data/canadian-motor-vehicle-traffic-collision-statistics/2021/canadian-motor-vehicle-traffic-collision-statistics-2021), both accessed 2026-10-15. These official publications differ for 2019 and 2021 occupant fatalities; they are retained as separate benchmark vintages, not competing targets to force into agreement. Precise operational treatment of unusual seating positions remains unverified. Broader commercial-vehicle victim totals in those publications cannot validate all victims of the road-tractor subset.
